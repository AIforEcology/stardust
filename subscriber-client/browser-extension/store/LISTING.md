# Store listing: Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons

Same package (`npm run build:store` → `stardust-extension.zip`) and same text for both stores.

**Name:** Stardust: AI Cost & Carbon Meter

**Category:** Productivity (Chrome: Tools)

**Short description** (132 characters max):
See the cost, electricity, carbon and water behind your ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini chats. Open source, by AI for Ecology.

**Single purpose** (Chrome asks for this):
Stardust shows the estimated cost and environmental impact (electricity, CO₂e, water) of the AI chat replies a user chooses to measure.

**Detailed description:**

Every AI reply uses electricity, water and money. Stardust makes that visible.

Turn Stardust on for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and each reply gets an indicator code on your toolbar, from A (lightest) to F (heaviest), with a breakdown of:
• Estimated cost in US dollars
• Electricity (Wh)
• Carbon emissions (grams CO₂e)
• Water (mL)
• A running total of everything you've measured

Private by design:
• Off until you turn it on, one site at a time.
• Only character counts and the model name leave your browser. Never the text of your messages or replies.
• No account, no name, no email. Each install gets an anonymous ID.

Honest about accuracy: chat sites don't show token counts, so browser figures are estimates and are labeled as such. Methodology and factors are public and versioned.

Stardust is free and open source (Apache-2.0), built by AI for Ecology (AIforE.org), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working on standard, transparent measurement of AI's environmental footprint. Code: github.com/AIforEcology/stardust
